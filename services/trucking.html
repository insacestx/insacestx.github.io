Frequently Asked Questions

What types of trucks can be covered?

We cover semi-trucks, box trucks, dump trucks, flatbeds, and specialized commercial vehicles.

Do I need cargo insurance?

If you transport goods for clients, cargo insurance protects you from liability if the load is damaged or lost.
