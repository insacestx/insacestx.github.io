Inland Marine

Protect tools, equipment, and mobile property while it's in transit or on job sites.

Overview

Inland Marine insurance helps cover movable business property that may not be fully protected under standard commercial property policies.

Coverage Highlights

  • Coverage for contractor tools and mobile equipment.
  • Protection for materials and inventory while in transit.
  • Coverage that can follow equipment between job sites.

Example Use Cases

  • A contractor insures power tools moved daily between multiple projects.
  • A business covers high-value equipment while transported across Texas.
  • A service company protects temporary equipment stored at customer locations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Inland Marine only for shipping companies?

No. It's commonly used by contractors, installers, and businesses with mobile tools or equipment.

Do I still need Commercial Property coverage?

Usually yes. Inland Marine and Commercial Property often work together to protect different property exposures.
