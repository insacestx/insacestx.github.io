Renters Insurance

Affordable protection for your belongings in a rented home or apartment.

Overview

Renters insurance helps cover your personal property and liability, even if you don't own the building.

Coverage Highlights

  • Coverage for personal belongings due to covered losses.
  • Liability protection if someone is injured in your rental.
  • Additional living expenses if you're temporarily displaced.

Why Choose ACES?

  • Bilingual support to answer your questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is renters insurance required?

While not required by law, many landlords require renters insurance as part of the lease agreement.

How much does renters insurance cost?

Renters insurance is typically very affordable—often less than the cost of a monthly streaming service.
