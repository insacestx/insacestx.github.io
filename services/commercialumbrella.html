Commercial Umbrella

Add higher liability limits across key business policies.

Overview

Commercial Umbrella insurance provides an extra layer of liability protection above underlying policies like General Liability, Commercial Auto, and Employers Liability.

Coverage Highlights

  • Higher liability limits for large or severe claims.
  • Can extend protection over multiple underlying business policies.
  • Helps protect business assets from catastrophic lawsuit exposure.

Example Use Cases

  • A contractor adds umbrella limits to support larger project requirements.
  • A transportation business increases protection after fleet expansion.
  • A business owner protects retained earnings from high-value liability claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Commercial Umbrella the same as General Liability?

No. It sits above General Liability and other primary policies to provide additional limits.

Who should consider this coverage?

Businesses with contracts, fleets, or public-facing operations often benefit from higher umbrella limits.
